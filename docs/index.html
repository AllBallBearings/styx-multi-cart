How it works

Clear it now, check out later

A few screens from the actual extension in action on amazon.com.

Step 1

Clear your Amazon cart

Open the Styx panel and hit Clear Amazon Cart. Styx works through the whole cart for you instead of making you delete items one at a time — and your Saved for Later is never touched. Free and unlimited, on every plan.

Step 2

Or save it for a later checkout

Before it clears anything, Styx offers to save the whole cart as a new cart in your Amazon account — a later checkout, waiting for you. Pick Save & Clear and everything that was in your cart is waiting for you afterwards. (You can also save without clearing, from the Amazon cart page.)

Step 3

And now you have carts

A floating Styx button rides along on Amazon — click it to pop open a draggable panel where every list shows up as a cart (click off it to tuck it away). Jump into any cart, or hit Go to Carts to manage them on Amazon, where Styx relabels the Lists page as Your Styx Carts.

Step 4

Send a cart to your Amazon cart

Open any cart and hit Send All to Amazon Cart to load the whole thing into your Amazon cart — Styx skips anything that's out of stock, and asks which edition you want for books.

Step 5

Add to a cart while you browse

On any product page, Styx puts a branded Add to a Styx cart button right next to Add to Cart — pick which cart it goes in, so building the right cart is as easy as buying.

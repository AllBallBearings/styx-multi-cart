Why Styx

Easily push different purchases through Checkout...just like in the real world

Amazon gives you one cart. Styx gives you as many as you need — and keeps them under your control.

🛒

Save named carts

Snapshot your active Amazon cart under any name you choose — "Birthday gifts," "Office supplies," "Wishlist." Free for 2 saved carts, or go Premium for up to 20.

Switch with one click

How it works

From Cart chaos to Checkout bliss

A few screens from the actual extension in action on amazon.com.

Step 1

Save your cart, give it a name

Open Styx next to your Amazon cart, type a name like "Birthday gifts" or "Office supplies," and click Save — everything in your current cart is snapshotted instantly.

Step 2

Empty your Amazon cart with one click

Hit Clear Cart in Styx and your Amazon cart empties — your saved cart stays safe while your live Amazon cart is ready for a fresh start.

Step 3

Create a new cart

Type a name for your next shopping list and click Save — Styx creates a brand-new saved cart alongside the ones you already have, ready to fill up.

Step 4

Switch carts or add items on the fly

Load any saved cart into your Amazon cart with one click, or keep browsing — when you hit Add to Cart, Styx intercepts and lets you route the item into whichever cart you're building.

Step 5

Pick exactly which cart gets the item

Choose any saved cart from the popup — or create a new one on the spot — and the item lands there, or in your live Amazon cart if you choose.

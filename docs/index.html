Free browser extension

All your Amazon lists,
now reusable carts.

Styx repurposes your Amazon wish lists as reusable carts. Build as many as you want, then move items between your carts and your Amazon cart in a single click. Your carts live in your Amazon account, so they follow you to every device.

Save your whole Amazon cart into a brand-new cart with one click, send any cart back to your Amazon cart, and clear your cart instantly. Styx even relabels Amazon's own Lists page as Your Styx Carts and adds a bold Save to a Cart button right next to Add to Cart.

Why Styx

Unlimited carts, one click each way

Amazon gives you a single cart and buries your lists. Styx turns those lists into as many reusable carts as you want, and makes moving items between them and your Amazon cart effortless.

🛒

Lists, reimagined as carts

Every Amazon wish list shows up as a cart in one panel — and Styx relabels Amazon's own Lists page to match. Your carts are backed by your Amazon account, so they sync across every device.

⚡

One click each way

Send All to Amazon Cart loads a whole cart into your Amazon cart at once, skipping anything out of stock. Or Save cart to a new list saves your entire Amazon cart into a brand-new cart — right from the cart page.

🧹

Clear & save-to-a-cart

Empty your Amazon cart in one click. And on any product page, a bold Save to a Cart button sits right next to Add to Cart, so filling a cart is as easy as buying.

How it works

Move items in and out of your carts

A few screens from the actual extension in action on amazon.com.

Step 1

All your carts in one panel

Open Styx and every Amazon list shows up as a cart. Jump into any of them, or hit Go to Carts to manage them on Amazon — where Styx relabels the Lists page as Your Styx Carts.

Step 2

Save your whole cart into a new cart

On the Amazon cart page, click Save cart to a new list. Styx builds a brand-new cart from everything in your Amazon cart — so you can empty it and shop something else without losing a thing.

Step 3

Send a cart to your Amazon cart

Open any cart and hit Send All to Amazon Cart to load the whole thing into your Amazon cart at once — Styx skips anything that's out of stock.

Step 4

Save to a cart while you browse

On any product page, Styx puts a bold Save to a Cart button right next to Add to Cart and clears your Amazon cart in one click — so building a cart is as easy as buying.

How it works

Move items in and out of your carts

A few screens from the actual extension in action on amazon.com.

Step 1

All your carts in one floating panel

A floating Styx button rides along on Amazon — click it to pop open a draggable panel where every list shows up as a cart (click off it to tuck it away). Jump into any cart, or hit Go to Carts to manage them on Amazon, where Styx relabels the Lists page as Your Styx Carts.

Step 2

Save your whole cart into a new cart

On the Amazon cart page, click Save cart to a new list. Styx builds a brand-new cart from everything in your Amazon cart — so you can empty it and shop something else without losing a thing.

Step 3

Send a cart to your Amazon cart

Open any cart and hit Send All to Amazon Cart to load the whole thing into your Amazon cart at once — Styx skips anything that's out of stock.

Step 4

Add to a cart while you browse

On any product page, Styx puts a branded Add to a Styx cart button right next to Add to Cart — pick which cart it goes in, so building the right cart is as easy as buying.
